Loan Eligibility Criteria

Understand the requirements before applying

Age Requirement

Applicant must be between 21 and 60 years old at the time of application.

Minimum Income

Monthly income must be at least 15,000 to qualify for any loan type.

Credit Score

Minimum credit score of 650 required. Score of 750+ unlocks premium interest rates.

Debt-to-Income

Existing EMIs should not exceed 45% of your monthly income (DTI ratio).
