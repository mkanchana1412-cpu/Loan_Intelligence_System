How We Assess You

Transparent, AI-driven evaluation criteria designed to empower your financial journey.

Age Bracket

Applicants must be between **18 and 65 years** of age. This ensures financial stability and alignment with global retirement norms.

Minimum Income

A stable monthly income of at least **₹15,000** is required to ensure repayment capability across all loan types.

Credit Score (CIBIL)

We require a minimum credit score of **600**. Higher scores (750+) unlock premium interest rates and faster processing.

Debt-to-Income (DTI)

Your total existing EMI commitments should not exceed **45%** of your monthly gross income. This prevents over-leveraging.
